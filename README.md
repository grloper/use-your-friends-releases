## What is this game?

**Your friends are the tools.** Morph into a plank, a spring, a ball. Cross the facility on each other's bodies. Every puzzle needs 2+ living players.

![Blob researcher](images/blob_scared.png)

**The goo recruits the dead.** Your fallen friend comes back wrong - and hunts you.

![The Hollowed](images/lunge_moneyshot.png)

**It's listening.** Every monster is heard before it's seen. Watch what you say.

![Barnacle Choir](images/closeup_horn.png)
