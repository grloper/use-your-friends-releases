# USE YOUR FRIENDS v0.9.2 — Your own camera, your own controls

## Controls show only what you're holding

- Hints, menus, pause, results, replay, AI orders and the controls panel now show **only the device you're using**: keyboard keys, Xbox buttons (A/B/X/Y, LB/RB) or PlayStation buttons (Cross/Circle/Square/Triangle, L1/R1), and switch live when you pick up a different one.
- In-world prompts show each player's own button; no more "SPACE / A".

## Split-screen: your own third-person camera

- Every local player gets their **own third-person camera** in their own part of the screen (2 players side by side, 3–4 in quadrants). Movement follows your camera.
- Look around with the **right stick**, or the **mouse** for the WASD player (the cursor locks while playing and frees in menus). The arrow-keys player's camera swings behind their movement.
- Prefer the old view? **Settings → CAMERA → SHARED PARTY CAMERA**.
- Online: your movement is now sent in world space through your own camera, so it always goes where you point (this also fixes a direction mismatch between host and guest views).

## Fixes

- Sour Science Lab and Twin Tracks are clearly tagged **CO-OP · 2+ HUMANS**. A solo player who clears Pinball Palace sees "CAMPAIGN CLEARED — BRING A FRIEND" instead of being sent to levels AI friends can't carry.
- Twin Tracks' catapult now has its visible arm.
- Taffy Factory's hint teaches timing the presses instead of shoving friends under them.
