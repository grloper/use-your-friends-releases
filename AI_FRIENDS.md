# AI friends: solo co-op

AI friends are optional, local decision-driven companions—not online AI services. Join the lobby as a human, then add up to three AI friends to your four-player group. They use normal avatar physics, earned tools, jelly, Sour restrictions, deaths and checkpoint respawns.

## Controls

- Lobby: click **ADD AI FRIEND** / **REMOVE AI**, or keyboard **I** / **N**. On a gamepad: **Y** adds; **LB + Y** removes the last AI. Join your human controller first.
- Hold your ordinary Jump button to start with your human + AI group.
- During play: **F1** selects the next AI; **F2** follows; **F3** holds your current location; **F4** asks for nearby puzzle help.
- **F5** makes the selected AI a Plank where it currently stands; **F6** makes it Boing; **F7** returns it to Normal. Face the direction you want the tool to face. Locked tools stay locked.
- **F8** opens the paused orders panel. On a gamepad, pause with **Start**, then press **Y** for AI orders. Use the D-pad and **A** to issue an order, **Y** to select the next AI and **B** to go back. The panel also exposes earned Ball, Sticky and Balloon forms.

## Teamwork

**Hold Here:** stand on the pressure pad you want occupied, issue Hold Here, and move away while your AI walks onto the pad. The AI stays until you give another order. Once you are through a paired door, stand on the far-side plate and call Follow so your friend can pass too.

**Help:** chooses an accessible nearby linked pressure plate or scale counterweight for your human player. In Twin Tracks, AI respects its own colored lane and looks for plates that help your lane. Help holds its chosen target until another order; it does not abandon an opened door while you cross.

**Tools:** ask an AI to Hold Here near a gap or ledge, then select Plank or Boing. Plank can serve as a real bridge; other AI can walk across stable Plank/Boing bodies. You can carry or throw AI friends normally; held AI stops steering. Finite jelly still applies. Call Follow or Normal to release a placed tool.

**Follow:** attempts safe floor/low-step navigation, waits when a tall wall or unsupported gap requires assistance, and gathers inside the rocket area once its human leader arrives. Ordinary death triggers ordinary checkpoint respawn; AI does not teleport or skip progress.

## Limits

These are **command-assisted teammates, not autonomous puzzle solvers**. They do not independently sequence every portal/catapult/maze or guarantee full completion of all seven courses. Human positioning, carrying/throwing, tool placement and orders remain part of the co-op game. Complex vertical routes may require the same physical assistance a human teammate would need. There is no simulated microphone, generated conversation, cloud account or AI API bill.

Only the local game/online host manages AI. Online clients cannot add/remove/order the host's companions. Adding AI consumes real party slots; remove AI to free space for another human.
